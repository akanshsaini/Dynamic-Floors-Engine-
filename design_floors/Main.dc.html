Dynamic Floors
One loop, once a day
Every ad slot has a lowest price we will accept. This program sets those prices each afternoon, learns from what happened, and repeats.
1
Read
Google emails yesterday's results at about 4pm: what every slot earned, and where advertisers were actually willing to bid.
2
Decide
It starts from the price our team set by hand, then moves off it only where yesterday proved moving pays. See the demo alongside.
3
Upload
The new prices go to the dashboard on their own, and an email lands telling us what changed and why.
Tomorrow's report contains the results of today's prices — so step 1 is also the scorecard for step 2. Nothing is trusted until it has been round this loop at least once.
