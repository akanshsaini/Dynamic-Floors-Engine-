What stops it going wrong
Five checks, any one of which can halt it
Each of these exists because it already caught something real. Stopping is always the safe outcome — yesterday's prices simply stay live.
Is the report real?
A half-written export looks exactly like a demand collapse, and would drag every price down with it. Anything under 60% of normal size is refused.
Did revenue collapse?
Compared against what that weekday normally earns, not a flat number. A genuine drop two days running resets everything to the human prices.
Is this change real?
Prices identical to what is already live are dropped, so the daily change list only contains things that actually changed.
How much may move today?
At most 30% of slots, biggest impact first. So it is a priority queue as much as a limit — the valuable changes always ship first.
Did yesterday's change hurt?
Any slot that fell while the rest of its site held steady gets yesterday's price back automatically. Compared against its own site, not against zero — when a whole site drops together that is a traffic event, and reverting there would throw away good prices.
